# Experiment Plan: Validation-Gated Hessian-Aware Ternary Refinement

## Research question

Can PTQ ternarization improve unseen activation-weighted reconstruction error by updating the discrete ternary assignment `T`, while preventing calibration overfit through a disjoint validation acceptance gate?

This is deliberately narrower than the previous rotation work. Hadamard, SSR modifications, block-coordinate competition, and multi-objective losses are excluded from this milestone. The only proposed contribution under test is validation-gated, Hessian-aware discrete `T` refinement.

## Hypothesis and causal contrast

- H1: fixed-`T` AGA leaves recoverable function-space error because it only refits scale and mean.
- H2: unconstrained `T` updates improve calibration fit but can overfit.
- H3: proposing changes with a fit covariance and accepting them only when they also improve a disjoint validation covariance yields better generalization to untouched test activations.

The decisive contrast is `gated_refine` versus both `fixed_t_allcal` and `ungated_refine`. A result against ITF alone is insufficient.

## R042 mechanism screen

- Model: LLaMA-2-7B-HF, FP16 source weights.
- Layers: 0, 10, 20, 31.
- Projections: q/k/v/o and up/gate/down.
- Blocks: two deterministic 128-column blocks per projection; 56 blocks total.
- Calibration source: WikiText2, seed 0, 12 sequences of length 2048.
- Activation subsampling: 128 deterministic positions per sample and module.
- Split by complete sequence: fit 6, validation 3, untouched test 3.
- Discrete search: at most four sensitivity-ranked single-coordinate proposals per output row; a coordinate is proposed at most once.

Variants:

1. `itf`: weight-space iterative ternarization.
2. `fixed_t_fit`: fixed `T`, activation-grid fit on proposal split.
3. `fixed_t_allcal`: fixed `T`, activation-grid fit on fit+validation; primary capacity-matched baseline.
4. `ungated_refine`: `T` refinement on fit+validation without a gate; overfit control.
5. `gated_refine`: proposals on fit, accepted only if both fit and validation row loss decrease.

Primary metric: activation-weighted NMSE on untouched test activations. Secondary diagnostics: fit/validation NMSE, block win rate, changed-entry fraction, proposal acceptance rate, and degenerate-grid fallback count.

## Pre-registered decision gate

Proceed to full GPTQ/PPL integration only if all conditions hold:

- median test NMSE improvement of `gated_refine` over `fixed_t_allcal` is at least 2%;
- test block win rate is at least 65%;
- median gated improvement is no worse than ungated refinement.

If the gate fails, report the mechanism as unsupported and do not rescue it by adding rotations, extra losses, or a larger coordinate search. If it passes, R043 integrates exactly the accepted update into official GPTQ and compares matched no-SSR and SSR baselines on WikiText2/C4 PPL, runtime, memory, and effective bits.

## Integrity and reproducibility

- No test covariance enters fitting, proposal ranking, acceptance, early stopping, or hyperparameter selection.
- Gate thresholds were fixed before reading R042 results.
- Seed, sample split, block locations, code revision, environment witness, raw per-block metrics, logs, and summary are retained.
- A synthetic unit test verifies that gated refinement never worsens its validation objective and that activation-grid fitting is non-increasing on its target covariance.
